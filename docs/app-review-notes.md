## 2. Purpose and audience

Sheafnote is a handwritten notes app for iPad and iPhone. You write with Apple Pencil or a finger in notebooks
that look and behave like paper ones. It is for students and anyone who takes notes by hand: class notes,
annotating lecture PDFs, and studying from their own notes with flashcards and review tape. It keeps
handwriting on the device, searchable and organized in folders, without an account.

## 3. How to use the main features

No login, account or sample files are needed. Everything works offline from first launch.

- Notebooks: tap + in the library to create a notebook, pick a cover and a paper template (ruled, grid,
  dotted, Cornell, planner and more), then write with Apple Pencil or a finger.
- Editing: pen, highlighter and erasers in the toolbar; lasso to select, move and copy writing; shapes,
  text boxes, images, stickers and links from the toolbar.
- PDFs: import a PDF from Files (or open it in Sheafnote from another app) and write on its pages.
- Search: the Search tab finds notebook titles and handwritten words. Handwriting is recognized on the device.
- Study: draw a flashcard from a lasso selection and review it in Study; review tape hides parts of a page
  until you tap them.
- Audio: record audio while writing and play it back from the notebook.
- Lock: lock a notebook with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode.
- Export and backup: export notebooks as PDF or images from the share menu; back up the library to a
  .sheafnote file in Settings > Import & Backup.
- iCloud sync (optional): Settings > iCloud Sync keeps notebooks in step across the user's own devices.

## 4. External services

- Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database), only when the user turns on iCloud sync. Data goes to the user's
  own iCloud account.
- No other services: no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, AI services, payments or sign-in.
  Handwriting recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

## 5. Regional differences

None. The app works the same in all regions.

## 6. Regulated industry or third-party material

Not applicable. Sheafnote is not in a regulated industry and includes no protected third-party material.
Users import only their own files.
