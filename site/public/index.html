Notes for iPad and iPhone

Sheafnote

Handwritten notebooks, PDFs and study tools in one calm app. Free, with no ads and no account.

Coming soonto the App Store Open source, MITView on GitHub In beta testing now.

A shelf for every class and project

Notebooks with real covers, colored folders, favorites and a Recently Deleted bin, so nothing is ever lost by accident.

  • 20 covers, from plain cloth to patterns
  • Folders inside folders
  • Sort by name, date created or edited

Writes like paper

Pen, highlighter, eraser, shapes and lasso, built on Apple's PencilKit, so the ink feels the same as Apple Notes. Pick the paper for each page.

  • Ruled, grid, dotted, Cornell, music and planner pages
  • Lasso to move, resize, recolor or turn ink into text
  • Double-tap Apple Pencil to switch to the eraser

Search your handwriting

Sheafnote reads your writing on the device and makes it searchable, along with PDF text and typed notes. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Finds words in handwriting, PDFs and text boxes
  • Jump straight to the page
  • Bookmarks and an outline for long notebooks

Study from your own notes

Turn any part of a page into a flashcard and review it with spaced repetition. Cover answers with study tape, or record a lecture alongside what you write.

  • Flashcards with Again, Hard, Good and Easy
  • Tap-to-reveal study tape
  • Record audio, then tap any line to hear what was said as you wrote it

Start any notebook in seconds

Choose a cover, a paper style and color, and a size. PDFs and images can become notebooks too, then be annotated and exported again.

  • A4, US Letter, A5 or square
  • White, yellow or dark paper
  • Export to PDF or images
