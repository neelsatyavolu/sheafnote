Pens, paper and search for your notes. Every tool works offline.

Six paper styles for Apple Pencil

Ruled, grid, dotted, Cornell, music and planner pages, with Apple's PencilKit pen, highlighter and lasso.

Search inside handwritten notes

Writing is read on the device, so a search for a word finds the page you wrote it on.

Flashcards made from your notes

Review with Again, Hard, Good and Easy, or record a lecture and tap a line to hear it.

New notebooks in A4, A5 or Letter

Pick a cover, a paper style and a size, square included, on white, yellow or dark paper.

Your notes stay yours

Notebooks live on your iPad and iPhone, and sync only through your own iCloud.

An app with no account

Open the app and start writing. There is nothing to sign up for.

Notes with no ads or analytics

Sheafnote sends nothing about you anywhere.

Handwritten notes read on the device

Search runs on your iPad or iPhone, never on a server.

The same notebooks on iPhone.

The library and the pen tools are laid out for one hand, in light or dark mode. Every device on your Apple Account stays in step.

Read how sync works

PDFs in, PDFs and images out.

Open a PDF or photos as a notebook and write over them. Export pages as PDF, PNG or JPEG, or back up the whole library to one file.
